Geometric Reference & Sketching Workspace

Every Great Sketch Starts With Understanding.

GeoSketch helps artists, illustrators, and architectural sketchers dissect the underlying geometry of any reference image. Overlay customizable grids, calibrate scales, compare line ratios, construct perspective shapes, inspect smart geometric annotations, and measure angles without altering your original image.

Precision Toolset

Built for geometric observation, not generic photo editing.

Every measurement is calculated strictly in image-space coordinates while overlays adapt cleanly to your screen zoom.

Visual Guides & Grids

Overlay configurable row/column or cell-sized grids, center lines, diagonals, rule of thirds, golden ratio, symmetry axes, and custom movable guide lines.

Line & Ratio Calculator

Measure lengths, horizontal/vertical spans, and angles. Compare two or more lines live with simplified integer ratios, decimal ratios, and percentage breakdowns.

Shape Construction & Homography

Construct rectangles, squares, circles, ellipses, triangles, regular polygons, custom polygons, and freehand contours with affine transforms and 4-corner perspective homography.

Smart Visual Annotations

Inspect geometric structure directly on canvas—circle radius/diameter, ellipse major/minor axes, triangle altitude height, rectangle diagonals, and independent label visibility toggles.

Angle & Scale Calibration

Measure 3-point angles or intersecting lines (interior, exterior, acute, obtuse, reflex, CW/CCW) and calibrate pixel distances to mm, cm, m, in, or ft.

Snapping & Zoom-Adaptive Rendering

Snap to grids, endpoints, centers, intersections, and H/V alignments while zooming from 5% to 3200% with screen-constant hairlines, handles, and typography.

Studio Workflow

From reference photograph to proportional drawing plan.

Keep your original image untouched while building a clear geometric scaffold on independent layers.

01

Load Your Reference

Drop, browse, or paste a PNG, JPG, or WebP reference, or open an autosaved project stored locally in IndexedDB.

02

Frame & Calibrate

Apply non-destructive crop and orientation presets, turn on composition grids, and optionally calibrate a reference dimension.

03

Analyze Proportions

Measure key landmarks with snapped lines, angles, and perspective shapes while inspecting live ratios and smart annotations.

04

Sketch & Export

Work alongside the workspace or export an annotated PNG overlay, a CSV measurement table, or a full JSON project file.

Open Source & Local-First

Pure HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla ES Modules.

GeoSketch runs entirely in your browser with zero external backend dependencies. Projects and reference images stay locally in your browser's IndexedDB, and the full codebase is open source on GitHub.
